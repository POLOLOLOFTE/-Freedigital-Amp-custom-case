# -Freedigital-Amp-custom-case

the compatible case with the #Free digital amp and:
It's customizable!! just print on paper the design that you want and slap it on the front pannel!

**MOUNTING:**

-take the first piece and install the brass insert in the hole (by using a welder or heat) on the bottom that is uncentered and hasn't got supports

-than take the pcb (it's better if it's arledy tested) and try to put it in though the thing in red and the thing in blue(this process can be difficult i also suggest for adjust the clerace with the support cilinders on the bottom you can use a soder iron to smooth them out):

-after this you can screw in the the board(don't worry about the insolation i arledy think about that)

-now you gotta slide in from the top the face(if your potenziometers have knobs i suggest to take them out for better clerance)

-and now just put on the top pannel (it hasn't got any laching system)

-opsional: print any kind of design 50x125mm and glue itto the front

*it's raccomanded to have at hand some hot glue and soder iron to in case adjust clerance and stop part from wiggle*

**3d PRINT:**

it's preatty flexible the choosing of the material, just not rubber like materials off course and materials that dose not melt at 60*C, it should be safe at 80*C

it need some kind of precision: around 0.2mm; and it's almost obbligatory to use supports.

**remember that it's untested**

**COOL THINGS:**

-this project will be correlated to the next ones so the case is scalable so you can put multiple amplificator on top of another

-the front pannel is removable by taking out the top and slide it out and mabey even dismount the knobs of the potenziometers so you can print on a normal printer a custom design and stick it to the front!
